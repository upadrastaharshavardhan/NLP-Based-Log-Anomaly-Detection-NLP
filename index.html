01 — The Problem

Logs contain signals. Finding them manually doesn't scale.

Modern systems generate huge quantities of textual operational data. Traditional rule-based monitoring can struggle with changing formats, noisy messages, and previously unseen patterns.

📜

Unstructured Logs

Operational messages contain valuable information but often arrive as semi-structured or natural-language text.

⚡

Alert Fatigue

Excessive false positives can make it difficult for engineers to identify the events that actually matter.

🔎

Hidden Anomalies

Important abnormal behavior may not always match a predefined keyword or static rule.
